Research Gaps & Underserved Groups

The following populations are underrepresented in current research and should be prioritized in future studies:

  • Transgender and LGBTQ+ veterans
  • Veterans aged 55+ (underrepresented in most studies)
  • Veterans with cognitive disabilities
  • Assistive technology users (screen reader, magnification, voice input)
  • Other-than-honorable discharge status
  • Immigrant-origin and expat veterans
  • Asian, biracial, and Native American veterans
  • Veterans using mobile devices primarily

Recommended Follow-up Research

  • Accessibility testing with assistive technology users across all product phases
  • Mobile-first usability testing
  • Research with underserved demographic groups
  • Long-term engagement research
  • Transgender veteran experience across all phases

About This Research

Synthesis Period: 2019-2026

Number of Studies: 50+

Primary Research Methods: Semi-structured interviews, usability testing, contextual inquiry, scenario-based testing, SME interviews

Sample Sizes: 8-16 veterans per study

Diversity Focus: Rural, urban, varying education levels, multiple racial/ethnic groups, underserved populations
