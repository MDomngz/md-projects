Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to have a specific research topic in mind? ▼

No, but having one helps. If you don't have a topic, we suggest exploring a research question relevant to your current work or choosing a hypothetical scenario (e.g., "How do users experience payment forms in mobile apps?"). The exercises are designed to work with any UX research context.

Can I skip days or do this at my own pace? ▼

Absolutely. The program is structured for 30 days, but you can move through it at your own pace. Exercises build on each other (especially across weeks), so we recommend maintaining the week-by-week sequence.

What AI tools do I need access to? ▼

The program uses tools with free or freemium tiers: ChatGPT (free tier available), Claude, and Perplexity. You can complete the program without paid subscriptions, though advanced features (like GPT-4) may enhance some exercises. All prompts are tool-agnostic and adaptable.

How much does this cost? ▼

This program is completely free. We believe research skills and AI literacy should be accessible to everyone. AI tools have their own pricing, but free options are available for the entire 30 days.

Can I use this case study in my portfolio or job interviews? ▼

Yes! The entire point is to build artifacts you can showcase. Your case study, prompt library, and methodology guide are yours to share. They demonstrate research thinking, AI fluency, and ability to deliver structured work—all attractive to employers and clients.

What if I'm new to research or AI? ▼

Perfect—this program is designed for learners at all levels. Week 1 covers fundamentals, and each exercise includes clear explanations and example prompts. You'll learn by doing, which is the most effective way to develop both research and AI skills.

How is this different from other AI or research courses? ▼

This program is project-based and practical. Rather than abstract lessons, you conduct actual research and use AI to enhance real analysis. By day 30, you have a complete, portfolio-ready artifact—not just knowledge, but demonstrable work. It's also built on accessibility and clarity principles, making AI research approachable for everyone.
