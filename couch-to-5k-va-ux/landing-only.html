The 30-day program

Four weeks. Four skill levels.

Skills build on each other. By the end, you'll be using AI across every phase of the design process.

Week 1

Foundations & First Prompts

Get comfortable talking to AI. Learn how prompt structure, context, and specificity change everything.

  • Write your first prompt using a VA scenario
  • Rewrite a vague prompt into a great one
  • Ask AI to explain a VA form pattern
  • Use AI to draft stakeholder talking points
  • Practice giving AI examples of good output
Week 2

Research & Discovery

Put AI to work on the messy middle of research: synthesis, affinity mapping, and finding what you missed.

  • Synthesize notes from a Veteran interview
  • Generate an affinity map from session quotes
  • Draft a research plan with AI as thought partner
  • Identify gaps in your screener questions
  • Turn findings into a shareable summary
Week 3

Design & Iteration

Use AI to accelerate design work: critique flows, audit content, generate alternatives, and stress-test decisions.

  • Get a design critique from AI using VADS principles
  • Audit a page for plain language compliance
  • Generate three alternative microcopy options
  • Map edge cases for a form flow
  • Create an accessibility review checklist
Week 4

Advanced Workflows

Chain techniques together. Build reusable workflows and integrate AI into your daily design process.

  • Build a reusable prompt template for your team
  • Use AI to prep for a Collaboration Cycle review
  • Draft a product brief with AI as co-author
  • Create a research repository entry from raw notes
  • Design your personal AI workflow toolkit

Day 11 — Synthesize a research session

~8 min
1
Read the context

A quick paragraph explaining why research synthesis is one of the highest-value uses of AI for VA researchers, and how to handle PII safely.

2
Copy the prompt

A ready-to-use prompt that asks AI to identify key themes, group related quotes, flag contradictions, and suggest follow-up questions from your session notes.

3
Try it on your work

Paste in de-identified notes from a recent Veteran interview. Review what AI surfaces. Compare it to your own synthesis. Notice what it caught that you missed.

4
Reflect and save

One question to consider: "What would change if you had this synthesis 30 minutes after every session?" Save the prompt if it worked for you.
